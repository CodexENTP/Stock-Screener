Scan settings

Every scan covers NYSE and NASDAQ stocks with positive net income, at least 250K average daily volume, and your price range. Matches are then scored (Codex score) and rated for volatility. Daily prices come from Business Quant, 20 stocks per call: Quick Scan uses about 3 calls, Standard 5, Deep 13 and Maximum 20. Choosing the 4H chart also uses 1 Twelve Data credit per stock that passes the price and volume checks, at about 8 stocks per minute, so 4H scans take longer. A batch usually finds a handful of stocks, and results add up as you scan more batches.

How each number is calculated
Prices, volume and the market check use end-of-day data from Business Quant, so they reflect the last completed trading day. The 4H entry check and the charts use Twelve Data and include the current session.
Net income is Business Quant's annual figure and must be positive. Sector comes from Business Quant. Average volume is the 20-day average and must be at least 250K.
RSI uses 14 periods (Wilder). MACD is 12/26/9. The MACD bullish crossover is the MACD line crossing above its signal line. The RSI bullish crossover is RSI crossing above its own 9-period average, above 30, or above 50, depending on your choice. Ichimoku uses the standard 9, 26 and 52 periods, with the future cloud taken from the leading spans projected 26 candles ahead and the lagging span compared with price 26 candles back. 4H signals use Twelve Data and include the current session. Keltner Channels are a 20-period EMA with 2 times a 10-period ATR.
Volatility tiers use the standard deviation of daily moves over about 3 months: under 1% Ultra Stable, 1 to 1.8% Stable, 1.8 to 2.8% Neutral, 2.8 to 4% Volatile, above 4% Aggressive.
Typical 5-day move is that daily deviation scaled to 5 days (one standard deviation, so roughly two-thirds of 5-day moves fall inside it). It is an estimate from history, not a forecast. 3M drift is the price change over the last 63 trading days.
Candlestick patterns are rule-based checks on the most recent daily candles, so treat them as prompts to look at the chart.
Good and bad signals are listed from most to least important. The importance numbers are my own judgement, not an industry standard. Good: golden cross 95 (just crossed) or 75 (within 10 candles), Morning Star 70, Bullish Engulfing 65, MACD bullish crossover 60, golden cross approaching 60, Piercing Line 55, bouncing off support 55, Hammer 50, RSI oversold 50, RSI bullish crossover 45, MACD approaching 40, volume surge 35, RSI approaching 30. Bad: death cross 100 (within 10 candles), death cross approaching 80, breaking support 75, Evening Star 70, Bearish Engulfing 65, Dark Cloud Cover 55, Shooting Star 50, MACD bearish crossover 50, thin volume 45, MACD weakening 40. Golden and death crosses compare the 50 and 200 period averages on the daily chart. A death cross lowers the Codex score by 25 points, or 15 if it is only approaching, and a golden cross adds to the indicator part of the score.
